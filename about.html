Our story

A house on a single mountain.

Harvest Deli is a small Greek producer of single-origin honey, olive oil and mountain tea. What we ship is small by nature, a few hundred jars a season, gathered slowly and sealed by hand.

We work with olive groves and wild tea hillsides in the same spirit: little, seasonal and honest.
