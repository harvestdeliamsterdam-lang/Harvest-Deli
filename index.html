The Origin

Born in the quiet hills of Northern Greece.

A thousand hives stand across the limestone ridges of the Greek mountains. Wild thyme, heather and arbutus bloom in a single, untranslatable season. The honey is shaped by this land, and by nothing else.

Greece · Spring harvest
The Process

A practice refined by time, not technology.

01

Wild Harvest

Combs gathered by hand from hives set high among untended wildflowers — never near a road, never near a field.

02

Cold Extraction

Spun slowly at the temperature of the cellar, never heated. Every enzyme and trace of pollen is left intact.

03

Natural Resting

Settled for fourteen quiet days in oak. Nothing forced, nothing filtered — the texture left fully alive.

Response time — usually within 24 hours

Cannot find your question? Write to hello@harvestdeli.nl and we reply within two business days. For order matters, orders@harvestdeli.nl.

  • Orders leave our Dutch depot within three working days. From dispatch you can expect one to two working days inside the Netherlands and Belgium. Track and trace is shared by e-mail. Full breakdown on the shipping page.

  • The Netherlands and Belgium, with a limited number of other destinations available at checkout. Shipping is complimentary within the Netherlands & Belgium above €65.

  • Yes. Combs are spun at cellar temperature, never above eighteen degrees. The honey is then settled, never filtered, for fourteen days in oak. Every enzyme, every grain of pollen, every aromatic note of the season remains intact. Read more in the tasting essay.

  • Indefinitely, sealed. Honey contains almost no water and is naturally antibacterial. Store cool, dry and out of direct light. Crystallisation is natural and reversible: warm the jar in a water bath at no more than 35°C to return it to liquid without damaging it.

  • Within fourteen days of receipt you may return unopened, wax-sealed jars without giving a reason. Once the seal is broken, the jar cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Full instructions and the model form are on the returns page.

  • Yes. An oak-veneered presentation box, lined in linen, holds one to three jars and travels with a handwritten card. Select gift packaging in the checkout, with a small €9,50 surcharge.

  • Do not give honey to children under twelve months of age. This is the standard advice of the Dutch Voedingscentrum, due to a very small risk of infant botulism in any unprocessed honey. For everyone above one year, honey is safe to enjoy.

  • There is no public estate to visit. Harvest Deli is small; everything is gathered in Greece and shipped from our Amsterdam depot. For press or wholesale, write to hello@harvestdeli.nl.
